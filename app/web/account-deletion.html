Request account deletion

For ShoesERP, published by Aeonis Labs

Publisher location: Multan, Pakistan

How to submit a request

  1. Send an email to a3onislabs@gmail.com.
  2. Send it from the email address linked to your ShoesERP account. Include the workspace name if you know it.
  3. If you cannot access that email account, explain this in your message. We may ask for additional information to verify ownership.

Do not include your password, one-time verification code, recovery code, or another person's private or financial information.

Requests are reviewed manually. We aim to complete a verified request within 30 days and will contact you if verification or other information is needed.

What deletion covers

Once a request is verified, Aeonis Labs will remove the account and personal profile information that can be deleted without breaking workspace records or violating a legal obligation. Account deletion ends access to ShoesERP.

Shared workspace records are not deleted with an individual account. Invoices, receipts, transactions, shop balances, inventory and audit history may remain with the workspace for accounting, legal, security, and operational purposes. A name or user identifier may remain in a historical record to identify who created or changed it.

Deletion request and verification records are retained for 365 days after the request is closed, then deleted unless a longer period is required to resolve a dispute or meet a legal obligation.

Copies saved outside ShoesERP

Encrypted backup files saved to your phone or Google Drive are controlled by you and are not automatically deleted when your ShoesERP account is deleted. Remove those copies from your device storage or Drive yourself.
