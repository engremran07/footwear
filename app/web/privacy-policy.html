Privacy Policy

For ShoesERP, published by Aeonis Labs

Effective date: 30 October 2026

Account deletion: Request deletion of your ShoesERP account.

Who is responsible for your information?

ShoesERP is published by Aeonis Labs, located in Multan, Pakistan. For privacy questions or requests, email a3onislabs@gmail.com.

Workspace administrators manage access to their workspace and are responsible for the business information their members enter. Aeonis Labs operates the ShoesERP app and its supporting services.

Information ShoesERP processes

Depending on the features used by a workspace, ShoesERP processes:

  • Account information, including email address, display name, Firebase Authentication identifier, role, workspace membership, account status, and seller route assignments.
  • Workspace business information entered by members, including routes, shop names and contact details, shop addresses or coordinates, products, stock, inventory movements, invoices, receipts, transaction and balance records, reports, and settings.
  • Workspace branding, including company logos stored with workspace settings, and HTTPS product-image URLs supplied by workspace users.
  • In-app notification records and information needed to display them to authorized workspace members.
  • Technical diagnostics. On supported Android builds, Firebase Crashlytics may receive crash and error reports associated with the Firebase user identifier, role, workspace identifier, and app version.
  • On Android releases configured to use Play Integrity, Firebase App Check may receive app-integrity attestation information.
  • Encrypted backup archives when a user explicitly creates or restores a backup. A user may choose device storage or, when configured, their own Google Drive.

Shop location details are business data entered or managed by workspace members. ShoesERP does not collect device location in the background or request location permission at startup. Image and backup selection uses the operating system's file or image picker.

How information is used

Information is used to sign users in, apply workspace and role access, provide route, shop, inventory, invoice, ledger, reporting, notification, and backup features, protect service integrity, and diagnose technical failures. ShoesERP does not display advertising or use workspace financial records for advertising.

Workspace members are responsible for entering business information lawfully and for informing people whose shop or contact details they add when required.

Service providers and sharing

  • Google Firebase provides authentication, database, app-integrity checks when enabled, and crash diagnostics. Information is processed by Google to provide those services.
  • If a user chooses Google Drive backup, ShoesERP requests the drive.file scope and transfers an encrypted archive to or from that user's Drive. Backup is user-triggered, not background synchronization.
  • When an external product-image URL is loaded, the image host receives a network request and may receive ordinary connection information, such as an IP address.
  • If a user emails a privacy request, their email provider and the publisher's email provider handle that correspondence.

ShoesERP does not sell workspace business information. Workspace members may share information with other people in their workspace.

Backups

Backups are created only after a user action and are scoped according to the user's role and workspace access. Encrypted archives saved to a phone or the user's Google Drive are controlled by that user and are not automatically deleted by Aeonis Labs when an account is deleted. The user must remove those copies from the device or Drive.

Retention and account deletion

To request deletion of an account, use the public account-deletion request page or email a3onislabs@gmail.com. Requests are reviewed manually. We aim to complete a verified request within 30 days.

An individual account request does not delete shared workspace invoices, receipts, transactions, shop balances, inventory movements, or audit history. These records may be retained by the workspace for accounting, legal, security, and operational purposes and to preserve financial and inventory integrity. A user's name or identifier may remain in a historical record as its creator or actor.

A copy of the deletion request and related verification correspondence is retained for 365 days after the request is closed, then deleted unless it must be retained longer to resolve a dispute or meet a legal obligation. This period applies to request records, not to workspace business records or user-controlled backup copies.

Security

ShoesERP uses Firebase Authentication and tenant-scoped access controls to limit access to workspace data. No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. Do not share your password or verification codes with support.

Children

ShoesERP is a business tool intended for adult users. It is not designed for or directed to children under 18.

Policy changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Questions about this policy or a deletion request can be sent to a3onislabs@gmail.com.
